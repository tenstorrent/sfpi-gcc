sweep_ccmask_dirs.c — ccmask keep-mask equivalence, all four float
order directions vs +0.0, exhaustive over 2^32 lane encodings each.

The cut is the SETCC/COMPC lowering of a float compare against +0.0;
both it and the SFPGT/SFPLE mod1==8 SET_DEST keep-masks are modelled
with the reference simulator's semantics of SFPSETCC, SFPCOMPC,
SFPGT, SFPLE, SFPAND and sign_mag32_total_order.

Build and run: cc -O2 sweep_ccmask_dirs.c -o sweep && ./sweep

LE (x<=0)  mismatches=0  cut-stream=269ba7dc7bdddcda  hw-stream=269ba7dc7bdddcda  EQUAL
GT (x>0)   mismatches=0  cut-stream=7b9f655a7bdddcda  hw-stream=7b9f655a7bdddcda  EQUAL
LT (x<0)   mismatches=0  cut-stream=54a3e8b604222325  hw-stream=54a3e8b604222325  EQUAL
GE (x>=0)  mismatches=0  cut-stream=7d40380004222325  hw-stream=7d40380004222325  EQUAL
RESULT: EQUAL over 2^32 x 4 directions

Stream commitments are position-salted FNV-1a 64 over the per-x
expected keep-mask (cut) and computed keep-mask (hw); equality per
direction plus mismatches=0 is the proof condition.

LICENSES: the LT/GE (swapped-operand, writable-zero) arms of the
rvtt_ccmask fold; the same harness re-verifies the LE/GT arms.  Those
arms may fire only while this RESULT is EQUAL.
